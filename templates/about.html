About Me

This is what I do.

I’m Biniam Zemenfes, a Software Engineering student from Ethiopia with a strong interest in technology, software development, and building solutions that can make everyday life easier. I enjoy learning how technology works and turning ideas into practical applications. My journey in software engineering has given me the opportunity to explore different areas of programming while continuously improving my skills.

I work mainly with technologies such as Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Flask, and other modern development tools. Through personal and team projects, I have built applications ranging from management systems and web applications to real-time platforms. These projects have helped me understand not only how to write code, but also how to approach problems, design useful systems, and turn an idea into something people can actually use.

Beyond programming, I’m interested in entrepreneurship and creating technology that addresses real problems. I enjoy exploring ideas related to digital payments, healthcare, transportation, and other areas where software can have a practical impact. I believe that a good developer should not only know how to code, but should also understand the problem they are trying to solve and the people they are building for.

This blog is a place where I share my learning journey, projects, experiences, ideas, and lessons I discover along the way. I’m still learning and experimenting, and I created this space to document that process while hopefully helping other students and aspiring developers who are on a similar journey.
